## Full description (max. 4000)

```
For health-conscious shoppers: Macro Lens helps you make faster, better choices in the supermarket. The shelves are crowded, time in the aisle is short — the app filters in seconds. Deliberately no quantities and no tracking: a measuring device, not a diary.

Point the camera at a product, the barcode is scanned, and the four charts that matter are right there:

• Macro donut — where do the calories come from (protein, carbs, fat)?
• Calorie density — how much energy per 100 g?
• Protein density — how much protein per 100 kcal?
• Satiety — how filling per calorie? (estimate)

Swipe the column down and the next product appears beside it: up to four products side by side, with the best value per row marked. That is the point — the decision is made in the aisle, in seconds, without reading a label.

DELIBERATELY WITHOUT GRADES
No Nutri-Score, no A–E grade, no score. Four pictures say more than a letter — and the decision stays yours. If a value is missing from the database it is not invented: that chart is simply left out.

FEATURES
• Continuous scan with a live camera — just hold the barcode in view
• Compare up to four products side by side – swipe down to pin, swipe up to remove
• Unpackaged basics list: around 65 staple foods, fully offline and directly comparable
• Name search through Open Food Facts
• History with search
• Offline cache: products you scanned once stay available
• German and English, light and dark

PRIVATE
No account, no cloud, no ads, no telemetry. Everything stays on the device. The only thing that leaves it is the barcode, sent to the open Open Food Facts database.

DATA
Nutrition: Open Food Facts (ODbL). Basics: USDA FoodData Central (public domain). Barcode scanning runs on-device (ML Kit).

Note: satiety is an estimate. Macro Lens is an everyday helper, not medical advice.
```
