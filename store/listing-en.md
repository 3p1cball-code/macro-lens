## Full description (max. 4000)

```
For health-conscious shoppers: Macro Lens helps you make faster, better choices in the supermarket. The shelves are crowded, time in the aisle is short — the app filters in seconds. Deliberately no quantities and no tracking: a measuring device, not a diary.

Point the camera at a product, the barcode is scanned, and the charts are right there. Swipe the column down and the next product appears beside it: up to four products side by side, with the best value per row marked. The decision is made in the aisle, without reading a label.

YOUR HOME SCREEN
By default four charts: macro donut (where the calories come from), calorie density, protein per 100 kcal and satiety per calorie. In the settings you build your own screen from up to five of ten building blocks – by drag & drop:
• Macros (optionally with sugar as its own slice)
• Calorie density · Protein · Satiety
• Sugar · Fibre · Salt · Saturated fat
• Fat quality – how much of the fat is saturated
• Traffic light – fat, saturates, sugar, salt at a glance
Every block has its own settings, for example per 100 g, per serving or per pack. Five ready-made presets, save your own.

ALLERGENS & INTOLERANCES
Pick your allergies (the 14 major EU allergens, e.g. gluten, milk, nuts, peanuts, soy, egg) and intolerances (lactose, fructose, sorbitol, caffeine, histamine). If a scanned product contains any of them, it is highlighted red or orange, the phone vibrates, and the reason is shown at the top. Traces can be reported too. The check is a help, not a guarantee: if the ingredient list is missing it says "not checked" – never "safe". With allergies, always read the label.

NOT FOOD?
Detergent, shampoo and the like are recognised via Open Products Facts and Open Beauty Facts – and the app tells you, with a wink, that you'd better not eat them.

DELIBERATELY WITHOUT GRADES
No Nutri-Score, no A–E grade, no overall score. The traffic light shows single lights, never a grade. If a value is missing from the database it is not invented – it is simply left out.

FEATURES
• Continuous scan with a live camera
• Up to four products side by side – swipe down to pin, up to remove
• Per 100 g, per serving or per pack
• Unpackaged basics: around 65 staple foods, fully offline
• Name search through Open Food Facts
• History, offline cache for scanned products
• German and English, light and dark

PRIVATE
No account, no cloud, no ads, no telemetry. Your allergen selection, history and presets stay on the device. The only things that leave it are the barcode number or your search term, sent to the open Open Food Facts databases.

DATA
Nutrition: Open Food Facts (ODbL). Basics: USDA FoodData Central. Non-food: Open Products Facts, Open Beauty Facts. Barcode scanning runs on-device.

NOTE
Macro Lens is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Satiety is an estimate; traffic-light colours follow the UK Food Standards Agency front-of-pack thresholds. With allergies, coeliac disease or intolerances always read the label and, if in doubt, seek medical advice.
```
